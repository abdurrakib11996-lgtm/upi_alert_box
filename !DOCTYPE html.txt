    <h1>Privacy Policy for UPI AlertBox</h1>
    <p><strong>Last updated: June 28, 2026</strong></p>

    <p>This Privacy Policy describes how <strong>UPI AlertBox</strong> collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application.</p>

    <h2>1. Data Collection and Usage</h2>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Local Processing:</strong> UPI AlertBox is designed to process financial notification alerts locally on your device.</li>
        <li><strong>Notification Access:</strong> The app utilizes the <code>NotificationListenerService</code> to detect incoming payment notifications from your selected UPI and banking applications.</li>
        <li><strong>Data Storage:</strong> All transaction details (sender name, amount, and timestamp) are stored exclusively in your device's local <code>SharedPreferences</code>. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal financial information to any external server or third-party database.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>2. Permissions</h2>
    <p>To provide its core functionality, the app requires the following permissions:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Notification Access:</strong> Required to read payment alerts from third-party apps.</li>
        <li><strong>Overlay/Display over other apps:</strong> Required to show fullscreen pop-up alerts during locked-screen transactions.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>3. Data Sharing</h2>
    <p>We do not share, sell, or disclose your data to any third party. Your information remains entirely within your control on your device.</p>
