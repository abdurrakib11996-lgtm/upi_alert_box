Privacy Policy

For Phone Box · Last updated: June 28, 2026

1. Overview

Phone Box helps shopkeepers receive instant voice confirmations for financial transactions. We prioritize your privacy and ensure your transaction data remains secure on your device.

🔒 Privacy Commitment: We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal financial information to any external server. All data processing happens locally on your device.

2. Data Collection & Permissions

To provide our services, the app requires the following permissions:

  • Notification Listener: Automatically reads incoming push notifications from banking apps to track transactions.
  • Push Notifications: Required on Android 13+ for continuous app alerts and status updates.
  • Display Overlay: Allows the app to show full-screen payment popup alerts over other apps.
  • Ignore Battery Optimization: Ensures the notification tracking service runs reliably in the background.
  • Full Screen Intent: Enables transaction reports to pop up immediately when you unlock your device.
  • Internet Access: Used for loading rewarded ads (AdMob/StartApp) and online payment validation.

Note: All transaction details are stored locally in your device's SharedPreferences.

3. Firebase Services

We use Firebase services to securely manage app updates and analyze app performance. Firebase may collect basic device data and usage statistics to ensure a stable and secure experience[cite: 8]. As Firebase is a service provided by Google, please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy for more details[cite: 8].

4. Data Usage

Your data is used solely to provide real-time voice alerts and transaction verification. We do not use your data for unauthorized profiling.
