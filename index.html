Privacy Policy

For UPI AlertBox · Last updated: June 28, 2026

1. Overview

UPI AlertBox is designed to help shopkeepers receive instant voice confirmations for financial transactions. We prioritize your privacy and ensure your transaction data remains secure on your device.

🔒 Privacy Commitment: We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal financial information to any external server. All data processing happens locally on your device.

2. Data Collection & Permissions

The app requires specific permissions to perform its core functions:

  • Notification Access: Used exclusively to detect incoming payment notifications from your UPI/banking apps.
  • Overlay (Display over other apps): Required to show instant, full-screen transaction alerts on your device.

Note: We do not store transaction history on any cloud database. All details (sender name, amount, timestamp) are stored in your device's local SharedPreferences only.

3. Data Usage

Your data is used solely to:

  • Provide real-time voice alerts for your transactions.
  • Show transaction details on your screen for quick verification.

We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or profiling.
